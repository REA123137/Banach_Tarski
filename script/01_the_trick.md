# 01 · The trick

**00:00 – 01:15** · 125 words · Iasmina 40 / Rym 85
**Purpose** — create a debt. No commentary, no reassurance, no “of course this is impossible”.

**Scenes** `S01Trick · S01Squeeze · S01GhostReplay · S01HandOff`

---

> **LIVE ACTION** — Iasmina, to camera. A bare table, well lit. One plain foam stress ball in front of her. Two seconds before she speaks.

**IASMINA**  Hi. I'm Iasmina, and I have always been fascinated by magic.
You think I have one sponge ball in front of me?

> **LIVE ACTION** — She picks it up and stuffs it into her other fist. The fist closes. One second.

**IASMINA**  *beat* — Think again.

> **LIVE ACTION** — The fist opens: two balls, identical, one in each palm. Hard cut, no music, no reaction shot.
> *silence, 2 s*

**IASMINA**  Think it's just a clever trick? Today we are going to prove it to you. Mathematically.

> **LIVE ACTION** — She holds both hands out to the lens, offering the balls, as if handing them through the screen. Hold. This gesture comes back at the very end of the video, with a square of chocolate.

> **LIVE ACTION** — Cut to Rym, matching frame and matching light. She mimics taking them from the lens, closes her hands, opens them: empty.

**RYM**  Now they're gone.
*beat* — But we're not here for the magic. We're here for the maths.
In 1924, two Polish mathematicians, Stefan Banach and Alfred Tarski, proved that what Iasmina just did is not a trick at all.

> **LIVE ACTION THEN ANIMATION** — Slow-motion replay of Iasmina's hands. As they close, the image dissolves from the real foam ball into a cloud of points. The cloud splits into five numbered groups, each rotates, and two clouds re-form. Dissolve back: two real foam balls in her palms. This is the door into the animated part of the video.

**RYM**  Five pieces. Each one is moved: you rotate it, you set it down somewhere else.
None is stretched. None is squashed. None is copied.
And at the end there are two balls. Each the size of the first.
*beat* — This is not a video edit. It is a theorem.

## Animation notes

**From the real object to the cloud, and back.** The whole shot lives on that dissolve: the foam ball you have just seen squashed between Iasmina's fingers becomes a cloud of two hundred thousand points, doubles, then becomes foam again. The viewer has touched the object with their eyes before it turned mathematical.

**The squeeze is not decoration.** It plants, visually, the prohibition that governs everything else. You show what is not allowed ten seconds before saying it is not allowed. — `S01Squeeze`

**Keep a ghost wireframe** where the original ball stood during the replay: the second ball is then visibly new. — `S01GhostReplay`

**The hand-off through the lens is the spine of the film.** Shoot it twice, in the same light: once with the balls, once with the chocolate, so the last shot rhymes with the first. — `S01HandOff` is the animated match-frame to cut the two plates against.

## Bridge out

> “It is a theorem from 1924, and I am going to prove it to you.” — straight to the statement, in Rym's voice. No “of course, this is impossible.” Never.
