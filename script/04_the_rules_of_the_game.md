# 04 · The rules of the game: a motion is a letter

**03:15 – 04:35** · 194 words · Iasmina 56 / Rym 138
**Purpose** — make the idea of chaining motions familiar, before any formalism.

**Scenes** `S04RubikWord · S04TwoRotations · S04Reduce · S04Ribbon`

---

> **ANIMATION** — A Rubik's cube. One face turns, then another. Underneath the cube, the moves write themselves side by side like a word growing longer.

**RYM**  You have already done this without noticing, with a Rubik's cube.
On a cube you have basic moves. You chain them together, and the sequence of moves you performed can be written down. That is a word.
Let us take exactly that idea, but simpler. I allow myself two moves only, and I give them letters for names.

> **ANIMATION** — Two curved arrows around a sphere: one rotation labelled a, another labelled b, about two different axes.

**IASMINA**  Move a. And move b.
Since these are rotations, each one can be done backwards. Turning the other way undoes what you just did. I call those a⁻¹ and b⁻¹.
So a sequence of motions is simply a word written with those four letters. For instance a b b a⁻¹: do a, then b, then b, then a backwards.

**RYM**  And there is exactly one rule in this game. One. But it matters enormously.
If I write a move and then immediately its inverse, the two cancel. I have done nothing.

> **ON SCREEN** — a a⁻¹ = e, where e is the empty word: doing nothing.

**RYM**  So whenever I write a word, I always simplify it first. a b b a⁻¹ a becomes a b b. A word in which nothing can cancel any more is called reduced.
That is it. You know the rules of the game. Two moves, their two inverses, and a letter that cancels its neighbour.

## Animation notes

**The cube unfolds into a ribbon.** The Rubik's cube turns, then its faces unroll into a paper ribbon where the moves are printed as on a typewriter roll. A letter cancelling its inverse is staged as a particle collision: the two letters slam together, one brief white flash, nothing left. Sound matters here: a mechanical click per letter, a clean silence for the cancellation. — `S04Ribbon`
