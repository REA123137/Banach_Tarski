> **ON SCREEN** — The statement writes itself in large type, one line at a time.

**RYM**  *slow down* — You can cut a solid ball into finitely many pieces, then, moving those pieces without ever deforming them, obtain two solid balls identical to the one you started with.

Three words in that sentence do all the work.

**Finitely**: five pieces are enough, and we will know that by the end.
**Moving**: you may rotate and translate. Nothing else.
**Identical**: the same radius, exactly. Not smaller, not approximately.

That is all the theorem says, and it is already enormous.
